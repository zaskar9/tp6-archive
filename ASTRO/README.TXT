ASTRO - ZODIAC AND ASCENDANT
============================
ASTRO.EXE    "PC Astro 1.0", 1 Sept 1993, text mode. No source.
             Asks for birth date (day.month) and time (hour.min)
             and prints zodiac sign and ascendant. Credits in the
             binary: Michael Gossniklaus (Syntax), Fabian Kristmann
             (Idee). Analysed by string extraction in Oct 2026.
ASTROGR.PAS  program Aszendent, 13 Oct 1993: graphical successor
             with a star field. Include STARZEIC.INC.
ASTROGR.EXE  build
STARS.PAS, STARUNIT.PAS (+ .TPU)   units for the star field
STARTEST.PAS unit test
