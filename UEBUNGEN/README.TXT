UEBUNGEN - EARLY EXERCISES
==========================
The oldest files in the archive, mostly text-mode exercises from
July 1992 to spring 1993, plus a few later one-off programs.

1992: ENTBRI (29 Jul, oldest), DREIECK, YEN, KREISRIN, RABA_II,
      DEVISEN, BOERSE, GUETER, KREIS,
      QUADRATI, VERSANDH, ZAHLEN, GEBURTST (Zeller's weekday
      formula), GAUSS
1993: KASSE, RECHNUNG, VIDEO (video rental), TURBOSCH (TurboSchool,
      51 KB, Jan 1993), PHANTOM, CHR, SCANCODE, BVB (ticket machine
      of the Basler Verkehrs-Betriebe), LUFTDRUC, TEST, DATNAM
1994: IDEALGEW (ideal weight), ERBE (inheritance demo)
1995: DNA.PAS (7 Apr 1995, the newest file in the archive)
