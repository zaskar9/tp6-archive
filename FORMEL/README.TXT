FORMEL - FORMELSAMMLUNG
=======================
Formula collection with menus and mouse, written 29 June to
1 July 1993. Shows geometry formulas (cylinder, prism, ...).

FORMEL.PAS    program Formelsammlung, with the includes OKAY GRAPHICS
              FORTRAN MACHRAHM BACKGROU COPYRIGH ZYLINDER PRISMA
              UNTERMEN UNITMENU FORMMENU (.INC)
FORMEL.EXE    build
MOUSE.PAS, WRITING.PAS   units (copies of UNITS)
PRISMA.DAT, ZYLIFORM.DAT   formula texts

OLD\  the two-day-older first version (FORMEL.PAS, OKAY.INC).
